Information Collection and Use

Information We DO NOT Collect

Your privacy is guaranteed. When you use L'Esprit Français, we do not collect any information, including but not limited to:

  • Personal Identifiable Information: We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, username, or account credentials. There is no sign-up process.
  • Location Data: The App does not access your device's GPS or network location.
  • Usage Analytics: We do not track which questions you answer, how long you play, or your quiz results.
  • Device Information: We do not collect technical details about your device type, OS version, or unique device identifiers (IMEI, advertising ID, etc.).
  • Cookies or Tracking Pixels: We do not use any modern tracking technologies for profiling or advertising.

How the App Works

L'Esprit Français is a self-contained application. Its core functionality implies that:

  • All quiz logic, questions, and scoring are processed locally on your device.
  • An internet connection is not required for the App to function.
  • The App does not communicate with any external servers or APIs.

Data Storage

The App does not store any persistent data, such as quiz history or user preferences. High scores are not saved between sessions; they are only kept in memory while the App is running.

Third-Party Services

ChronoSwift does not contain any third-party SDKs, analytics tools (like Google Analytics, Firebase, or Flurry), social media plugins, or advertisement networks.

Children's Privacy

Since L'Esprit Français does not collect any data from anyone, it is completely safe for use by children of all ages. We comply with COPPA and similar regulations by not collecting information from anyone, knowingly or unknowingly.
